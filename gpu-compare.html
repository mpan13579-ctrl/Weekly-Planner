Verdict — build the Dual RTX PRO 6000

192 GB runs the real models and several agents at once, it's ECC/workstation-grade for 24/7, you own and can upgrade it (the platform even scales to 768 GB), and it's less than half the cost of the DGX. The 5090 hits a hard 32 GB memory wall; the DGX is a ~$90k sealed appliance with power you won't use. If the 240V wiring + heat is a hassle, the 300 W Max-Q version gives the same 192 GB on a normal outlet with half the heat.
